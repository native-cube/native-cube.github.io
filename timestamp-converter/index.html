    Input formats and precision

    Conversions, comparisons, and clipboard output are generated on this device. Inputs stay in this browser tab.

    Unix seconds and milliseconds

    Unix time counts from 1 January 1970 at 00:00:00 UTC. Negative values describe earlier instants. One second is 1,000 milliseconds.

    Auto-detect reads numbers with 12 or more digits before the decimal as milliseconds, ignoring the sign and leading zeros. Choose a unit explicitly for ambiguous inputs, such as 1000 milliseconds.

    ISO 8601 and time zones

    Use Z, UTC, or an offset such as +05:30 with a date and time. A date alone is midnight UTC. Use seconds 00–59 and up to nine fractional digits.

    Paste a single timestamp value from JSON or logs. Quoted JSON strings are accepted.

    The display time zone uses the browser’s historical offset for the instant. Fractional seconds stay exact when converting or comparing.

    CloudWatch Logs bounds

    The converter sorts two instants and writes --start-time and --end-time in Unix milliseconds. For sub-millisecond inputs, it rounds the start down and the end up.

    Log ranges start at the Unix epoch or later. Add the copied flags to aws logs filter-log-events with your log group.
